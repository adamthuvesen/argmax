Calmer activity marks

1 — Contact sheet

Sidebar size is 16px; 13px is the transcript's launch row and the iOS chat list. The 6× column is where a lazy keyframe shows. Every candidate takes its colour from --accent-deep and the accent-mixed rest tone, never from a literal green — the default accent already is sage, and hard-coding it would break the other fourteen themes.

2 — The sidebar test

The real question. Six chats running at once, one selected, calm rows between them. Scan the columns with your eyes unfocused: the shipped nest relays four dots every 900ms, so a column of it is 67 gestures a minute per row. Try Phase → unison here — phasing keeps separate jobs legible on a fast mark, but on a slow breath it turns a tidy column into a shimmer, and agreeing is calmer than disagreeing.

3 — Filmstrip

One cycle sampled at eleven fixed points, paused. This is the only way to judge a loop on paper — an arbitrary frame tells you nothing, which is how a perfectly healthy nest photographs as an empty gutter. Read across: the distance between the brightest and dimmest frame is the amplitude, and the number of turning points is how often it asks for attention. nest advances through the app's own --working-nest-phase, so its four dots stay coherent.

4 — Cost and calm

Gestures per minute is the honest proxy for "chaotic": how many times a minute the mark asks for your attention. Every candidate animates only transform and opacity, so all of them stay on the compositor — the shipped nest's cost is its part count, not its properties.
