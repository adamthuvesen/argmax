One column, the selected variant. A quote that carries a list and a code span, a quote straight after a heading, a one-liner, a two paragraph quote, a nested quote, and a quote of the user's own words. A shape that only works on a three-line paragraph is not a shape.

What the hook rejects

Every commit message is checked against three rules before the hook lets it through:

  • no Co-Authored-By trailer naming an assistant
  • no 🤖 in the subject or body
  • the subject fits type(scope): message

So the failure you hit is the first rule, not the third. The trailer is added by the CLI, not by the prompt, which is why removing it from the message text did nothing.
