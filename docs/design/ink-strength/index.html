What the setting actually moves

Every ink token is declared as a color-mix against one endpoint the ladder picks. Below level 7 the endpoint is the page, so each token fades in proportion to its own distance from it and the gap between body text and a caption survives. Above level 7 the endpoint is the theme's extreme — pure black on paper, pure white on charcoal.

Emphasis rides --prose-ink-strong and body prose rides --prose-ink, so the two move together and bold never falls behind the paragraph it emphasises.
